# Mission 14, Cape Rainy Assault: the playtest bot's plan (tools\playtest.py, "plan:" in an order)
#
# Phase 1: a 48 km night canyon run under a 550 m ceiling (the radio says 600; the game's own
# limit, set with SetRestrictedAltitude, is 550), past about 70 sweeping searchlights; being
# lit fails the mission. The route (m14-route.csv) is the middle of the canyon at 510 m, from
# the pilot's terrain survey (m14-survey.csv, tools\route-plan.py); the searchlights' sweeps
# (m14-fans.csv, tools\beam-fans.py from a recording on Hard) let the pilot time, lower and
# shift itself past each beam as it flies. The refuelling at the start is skipped by the
# pilot on the tanker's first radio line.
# Phase 2: attack the air base. The landing team (Basilisk) cannot land while the six Apaches
# at the landing zone (02_en_AH64_00_*) are alive: "hostiles are still near the LZ", and about
# 3.5 minutes after the phase starts the mission fails ("taking too long... surrounded").
# Once they land, reinforcements ("zouen1/3": APCs, tanks, AA guns) arrive and must be
# destroyed in about 100 s. Patchy clouds at about 350-1050 m. "cloudbase 350" keeps ground
# attack runs under the clouds (the first theory for the targets that never lock). It got
# one SAM kill from 200 m, but SAM_2 and the reinforcement vehicles still did not lock from
# under the clouds, and low flying meets the AA guns. (A 330 m ceiling for the whole fight
# was tried and removed: it pushed a rolling plane into the ground.) Still open (workbench
# section 14, finding 11). Best so far (19 Oct 00:10): the Apaches fall to single LAAM shots,
# the team lands 1.5-2 min in, then the reinforcements cannot be locked and the plane is
# shot down.
file m14-survey.csv
file m14-route.csv
file m14-fans.csv
start:
    pilot nav load {missions}\m14-survey.csv 500
    pilot fans {missions}\m14-fans.csv
    pilot fly route {missions}\m14-route.csv ceiling 550 floor 40 maxspeed 650 navfloor 25 then attack
phase MissionPhase_02:
    pilot fly attack floor 120 ceiling 99999 cloudbase 350 focus ah64_00,zouen
retry MissionPhase_01 mission
retry MissionPhase_02 checkpoint

# Mission 04 Rescue (story, Ace; level ms04, the Space Elevator map, Selatapura)
#
# Phase 1 is a stealth run: "If you're spotted, the mission is over. Stay out of enemy radar.
# Use of weaponry is also strictly forbidden." The radar areas are 79 active spheres (3.5 to
# 10 km radius) sitting on the ground: measured 19 Oct, the plane at 167 m was "in radar"
# 5.97 km from the centre of a 6 km one and "spotted" 3 s later, so low flight does not get
# under them. The phase is cleared on entering "Phase01_Clear_Field", a 14 km sphere around
# (-230296, 7181) by the Space Elevator; the mission's own section triggers (Phase01_Sect1End,
# Phase01_branch_root, Phase01_Sect2End) lie on the way. m04-route.csv: 128 km around every
# active sphere with 500 m to spare, 110 m over the highest ground nearby
# (tools\route-avoid.py on m04-survey.csv and m04-radar.csv). No weapons: the route order
# does not fire.
file m04-survey.csv
file m04-route.csv
start:
    pilot nav load {missions}\m04-survey.csv 2000
    pilot fly route {missions}\m04-route.csv floor 40 maxspeed 650 navfloor 30 beams off then attack
retry MissionPhase_01 checkpoint
# The game restarts phase 1 by itself when the plane is spotted (no retry menu): the same orders again
phase MissionPhase_01:
    pilot nav load {missions}\m04-survey.csv 2000
    pilot fly route {missions}\m04-route.csv floor 40 maxspeed 650 navfloor 30 beams off then attack
# the checkpoint after the stealth run: destroy the anti-air around the space elevator
phase MissionPhase_02:
    pilot fly attack
# the drone phase (15 minutes): fight
phase MissionPhase_03:
    pilot fly attack
